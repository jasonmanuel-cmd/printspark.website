## 🎯 Brand Voice

**Personality Traits:**
- **Energetic** - Fast, responsive, always moving
- **Professional** - Quality work, reliable delivery
- **Friendly** - Approachable, helpful, human
- **Modern** - Up-to-date technology, contemporary design
- **Confident** - We know we're good at what we do

**Voice Guidelines:**
- ✅ DO: Use active voice, clear language, confident statements
- ✅ DO: Be enthusiastic about customer projects
- ✅ DO: Emphasize speed + quality together
- ❌ DON'T: Be overly casual or use too much slang
- ❌ DON'T: Make promises you can't keep
- ❌ DON'T: Use industry jargon without explanation

**Example Phrases:**
- "Let's bring your vision to life!"
- "Fast printing that looks amazing"
- "Your designs, perfectly printed"
- "From concept to delivery in days, not weeks"

## 📝 Typography

### Primary Font: **Inter**
```css
font-family: 'Inter', system-ui, sans-serif;
```
- **Use for:** Body text, UI elements, forms
- **Weights:** 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semibold), 700 (Bold)
- **Why:** Highly readable, modern, professional

### Headline Font: **Outfit** (or Inter Bold)
```css
font-family: 'Outfit', 'Inter', sans-serif;
```
- **Use for:** H1, H2, H3, important headlines
- **Weights:** 600 (Semibold), 700 (Bold), 800 (Extrabold)
- **Why:** Bold, confident, attention-grabbing

### Font Sizes

**Desktop:**
- H1: 3.5rem (56px) - Hero headlines
- H2: 2.5rem (40px) - Section headers
- H3: 1.875rem (30px) - Subsections
- H4: 1.5rem (24px) - Card titles
- Body: 1rem (16px) - Standard text
- Small: 0.875rem (14px) - Captions, labels

**Mobile:**
- H1: 2.5rem (40px)
- H2: 2rem (32px)
- H3: 1.5rem (24px)
- H4: 1.25rem (20px)
- Body: 1rem (16px)
- Small: 0.875rem (14px)

## 🖼️ Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
- **File:** `logo.svg`
- **Minimum size:** 120px width
- **Clear space:** Equal to height of lightning bolt on all sides
- **Backgrounds:** Use on white, cream, or dark navy only

### Logo Variations

**1. Stacked Logo** (`logo.svg`)
```
     ⚡
  PRINT
   SPARK
```
- Use when: Vertical space available (footers, mobile)
- Don't use: When width < 120px

**2. Horizontal Logo** (`logo-horizontal.svg`)
```
⚡ PRINTSPARK
```
- Use when: Headers, wide spaces, desktop navigation
- Don't use: When width < 200px

**3. Icon Only** (`icon.svg`)
```
⚡
```
- Use when: Favicon, app icons, very small spaces
- Don't use: As primary branding (needs company name nearby)

### Logo Don'ts
- ❌ Don't change colors
- ❌ Don't add effects (shadows, gradients)
- ❌ Don't rotate or distort
- ❌ Don't place on busy backgrounds
- ❌ Don't outline or add borders
